# Automated Social Media Poster – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

Automated Social Media Poster is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner,
and it posts videos the owner made to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and
Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.

**What it accesses.** When the owner signs in, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram
grant the tool permission to publish videos and read basic account details
(such as the account name) needed to post. It accesses no other data.

**What it stores.** Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's
own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for
advertising or analytics. No data is collected from anyone else.

**Third-party services.** This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you
agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and
Google's handling of data is covered by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). TikTok's handling
of data is covered by the [TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy),
and Instagram's by the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy).

**Revoking access and deleting data.** Access can be removed at any time from
[Google account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions),
TikTok's app settings, or Instagram's "Apps and websites" settings. Deleting
the stored tokens from the owner's computer removes all data the tool holds.
